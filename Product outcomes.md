# Product outcomes

- [x] **Local study library and subject organization** — The app runs in a computer browser and lets the student add PDFs, images, and typed ICSE Class 10 content to a subject-wise local library. Dedicated navigation covers Maths, Hindi, Physics, Computer, Chemistry, Biology, History/Civics, English Language, English Literature, and Geography. Students can keep adding material; 36 general quick-reference notes seed the ten subject areas.
- [x] **PDF and image indexing with visual source cards** — The app extracts searchable, page-numbered text from PDFs, runs bundled English OCR locally on images and scanned PDF pages, saves page thumbnails for the first 12 PDF pages, and shows matching uploaded images or PDF page previews next to answers when available. Hindi text can be typed; the included OCR model is English-only.
- [ ] **At least 1,000 subject-wise question-and-answer records** — Add at least 1,000 answer-backed Class 10 questions across Maths, Hindi, Physics, Computer, Chemistry, Biology, History/Civics, English Language, English Literature, and Geography. Target 100 questions for each existing subject section. Every question has a chapter/topic label and an explanatory answer; question counts are verified from the delivered data.
- [ ] **Past-year questions with answers and provenance** — Include questions adapted from verifiable publicly accessible past ICSE papers across the subject set. Mark adapted past-paper questions separately from original practice; retain their exam year, paper and page/question reference where available, source title and link, and an answer. Never invent or label an original practice question as PYQ; if a paper cannot be verified, keep that entry as original practice.
- [ ] **Question bank and separate study sections** — Add a dedicated Question bank option in the sidebar. Allow browsing/searching by subject and topic, filtering original practice versus past-paper-derived questions, viewing answers, and opening their cited sources. Keep uploaded files, the question bank, and the existing subject sections easy to access separately.
- [ ] **Explicit Chatbot option with stronger offline retrieval** — Add a clearly named Chatbot option in the sidebar. Answer questions by locally searching the question bank, starter references, and the student's own uploaded/indexed material. Display source titles, relevant page or paper references, PYQ year and web source link when available; say when no strong match is found. This is an offline retrieval chatbot, not a generative model that has been trained or fine-tuned.
- [ ] **Automatic indexing of newly added PDFs and images** — When the student adds a PDF or image, automatically extract its text or OCR it on-device and make the result available in chatbot retrieval without a separate re-upload. Keep extracted material grouped under its selected subject and show matching uploaded images or PDF page previews with answers when available. Preserve the existing offline English OCR behavior and add a bundled Hindi model only if it can be sourced and validated.
- [ ] **Windows desktop installer** — The user selected Windows desktop. Provide a directly installable Windows x64 `.exe` for the offline study app, including the Q&A bank and local OCR assets. Keep app data local on the computer, serve the packaged app only through a loopback listener, and do not require internet after installation for core study functions. This is not an Android APK.
- [x] **Offline persistence and browser installation** — The app stores study files, OCR text, local page previews, events, and chat history in this browser's IndexedDB/local storage. Its installable PWA caches the app build and bundled English OCR engine/model for offline use. Data is local to this browser profile and does not sync across devices.
- [ ] **Keep responsive behavior without altering the desktop design** — Preserve the established desktop Study Desk layout and styles while ensuring the added sidebar items, question-bank filters/cards, citations, and chatbot work on narrow/mobile screens.
